# emo-drink — skills profile

**What it is:** A wearable-health research study that links physiological signals from smartwatches to well-being, with companion mobile apps, a data API, survey tooling, and ML models. Built with an industry beverage partner in a research-lab setting.

**Sub-projects scanned:** ~9 — a Kotlin/Android app and a Swift/SwiftUI iOS app (both integrating the Garmin Health SDK), a Node data API, an Expo (React Native) survey app + a Node survey backend, an ML models repo, a local control dashboard, a conference poster, and offline SDK reference docs/tooling.

**Sankha's role & contribution:** Lead app developer. Solo on the Android app (~127 commits), iOS app (~30), Expo survey app (~61), survey-node backend (~53), and the central dashboard; co-developer on the data API (~52 of ~80, alongside one collaborator). The ML models repo was authored by other researchers.

**Collaborative?:** Yes — other authors include Lee Hyung Woon (API/models) and Soundarya Ramesh (models). Sankha focused on: the wearable-integrated mobile apps (both platforms), survey capture apps, the data/API layer, wearable-SDK/health-data plumbing, and CI release automation.
